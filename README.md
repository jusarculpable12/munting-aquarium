# Munting Aquarium

A cozy Filipino fish-raising simulation. Care for your aquarium and grow your little fish shop!

## Your goal

**Unlock every fish variant in the book.** Buy fish and breed healthy adult pairs to discover new colors. Unknown variants appear as black silhouettes until you collect them. Discoveries stay unlocked even after you sell a fish.

## How to play

1. **Care:** Tap the water to feed. Avoid overfeeding and change dirty water.
2. **Grow:** Earn visitor coins, sell adult fish, and buy fish, accessories, or tank upgrades.
3. **Discover:** Keep a healthy adult male and female of the same species with room for babies. Open the **Book** to track your collection!

Progress saves automatically in your browser. Fish care pauses while you’re away. Export a backup in Settings before clearing browser data.

## Built with

**Next.js · React · TypeScript · CSS · HTML Canvas** for the interface, aquarium, and animated fish. Saves use browser local storage.
